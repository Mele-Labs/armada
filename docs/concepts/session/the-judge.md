# The Judge

**Part of:** [Job](../session.md).

How a Job draws the [Judge](../judge.md): where its act stands, what the act shows, and where its notes land.
`judge.md` owns what the Judge reads, the model it runs on and the rules it keeps.
Nothing here runs on its own: a reading starts when a person presses the act.

## Where the act stands

| Place | Judges |
| --- | --- |
| A row in the Job's ledger | The artifact the row stands for |
| The head of the Thread tab | The Job as a whole |
| The plan card | The plan version waiting for approval |
| The head of a document | That document |
| The Cockpit's Judge tile | The Job as a whole |

A ledger row carries it for a plan, pull request, commit, file, walk page, document, sketch, command, subagent, issue and note.
A link, a Job and a closed item have nothing for the Judge to read and carry no act.

On an artifact, the press opens a small box, **Verify that**, with a line to name what to check.
On the whole Job it opens no box: it brings the Judge tab forward, which has one of its own.
Left empty, the Judge looks for what the person or the agent missed.
Once a reading has notes, the box also offers **Read the notes**, which opens the artifact at them.

## What the act shows

| Shows | Means |
| --- | --- |
| A mark that moves | The Judge is reading |
| A dot | The reading has notes the person has not seen |
| A check | The last reading was clean |

The dot clears when the notes are drawn on screen.
No count is drawn beside it.

## Where the notes land

A note is drawn inside the artifact, on the part it names, headed **You should know**.

| Artifact | Where it is drawn |
| --- | --- |
| The plan | Under the group or task it is about, on the plan card and in the plan in full; under the summary where it is about the whole plan |
| A pull request | Under the diff line, on its Files tab |
| A document | Under the paragraph, heading, list item or table row, in the reading layer; at the head of the page where it names none, or the page no longer says its words |
| The Job as a whole | On the Judge tab, each note saying where it is |
| Any other | Under its row in the Job's artifacts, each note saying where it is |

**A Judge on an artifact shows its results in that artifact, and the Judge of the whole Job, which has no artifact to stand in, opens a tab of its own.**
At its foot the Judge tab says what judging is and holds a small composer in the Judge's own hue, words only, which asks.
Above that it says what was asked, draws the shape of the reading while it runs, then the notes, a line saying nothing was missed, or why the reading failed.

A group of the plan that holds notes carries a mark, so a folded group still says it has some.
[In Bridge](in-bridge.md), *The plan card*, owns how the plan card draws them.

## In the thread

A finished reading is one quiet line in the thread, after the row that was last when it finished.
It names the artifact and whether the reading was clean, and a press on it opens the artifact at its notes, or the Judge tab for the whole Job.
**It raises no call.** Nothing about a finished Judge is a row in the Needs you panel, since it asks nothing of the person.

## Send and dismiss

**Send to Job** sends the note to the agent as the person's message, and the note folds to one muted line where it stood.
**Dismiss** folds it the same way and tells the agent nothing.
A reading that fails or cannot be read leaves the artifact as it was and says why in Fleet's words.
