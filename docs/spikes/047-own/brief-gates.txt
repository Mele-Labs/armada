Plan the work as your own workflow, with `record_plan`. Fleet follows your work by the plan, so its groups are the stretches of the work, in order. Each group ends on one gate, which is a fact Fleet checks itself and you do not mark. Write the gate at the very end of the group's `gives` line, in square brackets, for example `... [gate: checks_green]`. The gates you can pick are: `plan_approved` (the first group's gate, always: the person approves your plan), `walk_approved` (the person approves a walk you showed), `document_approved` (the person approves a document you wrote), `checks_green` (Fleet runs the repository's check for the files you touched, at the commit you pushed), `merged` (the person merges the pushed branch when you ask; there is no pull request here), and `none` (the group ends when the next one starts). You choose how many groups the work needs and which gate ends each. Give each group the `paths` it works in, from the repository root, because Fleet places your edits by them. If the work changes shape you may record the plan again.
