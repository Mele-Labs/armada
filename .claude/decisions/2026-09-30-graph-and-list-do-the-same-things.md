# Can Plan's Graph view offer less than its List view?

**Decided 2026-09-30.**

Add task and a group's asks were in Plan's List view only, and the owner found he could not do them from Graph: *"As a rule, graph/list should offer the same features. I should never have to go to list because I can't do something in graph view."*

**Chosen:** Graph and List offer the same acts. Anything a group card or a task row can do in List, a group or task in Graph can do too, through the group panel or the task panel. A new act on one view is built on both, or on neither.

**Cost he took:** every act is built twice, or through a panel both views open.

**Where it landed:** `plan/task-dock`.
