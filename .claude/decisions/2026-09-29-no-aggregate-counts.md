# Does a list carry a count of what it lists?

**Decided 2026-09-29.**

The group-boundary card on Plan and the Record's task sheet opened with
`Checks 7`, then a strip of seven marks, then `screens_test failed`, above the
seven Checks drawn one to a row. The owner, on the `7`:

> "We also dont need the count (7) of checks here. Please make a standing rule so
> that agents stop putting aggregate counts like this. They seem to love doing that"

and on the verdict beside it: *"We don't need the 'Screen tests failed' in this
because it is shown right below it."*

**Chosen:** no aggregate count beside what it counts. Draw the items and leave
the number out. A count appears only where the items don't (a folded list, a tab
strip naming a backlog), which is the Tabs rule in
`docs/contracts/design-system.md` already.

**Cost he took:** a folded card or a long list no longer states its size at a
glance where the items are also drawn.

**What it says about him:** a number that repeats what's on screen is noise, the
same as a sentence that repeats it. It's `default-to-no-text` applied to figures.
He's seen agents add these counts over and over, and wants it stopped at the
source rather than caught one screen at a time.

**Where it landed:** `docs/contracts/design-system.md` (the rule), the
`GroupBoundary` head on `bridge/record-from-its-board`, and
`.claude/skills/how-the-owner-decides/`.
