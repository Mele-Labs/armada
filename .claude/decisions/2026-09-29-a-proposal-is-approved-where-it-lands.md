# Where does a person accept or reject what Helm proposes on a Studio?

**Decided 2026-09-29.**

A proposed relation was drawn twice: dashed on the board with "proposed, waiting on you" under its label, and again in a "Waiting on you" card in the board's top-right corner, which carried the Accept and Reject. The owner, on the Board's legend Studio: *"This approval box is so disconnected from the node, the node already says proposed waiting on you above it … maybe the node has a header eyebrow that shows proposed and then a footer with the approve or reject buttons. This way I could see exactly where this note is going to be added on the board, and I can approve or reject it right there."*

**Chosen:** the approval moves onto the thing proposed, and the corner card goes. A proposed relation's label on the board becomes a small card: who proposed it as the eyebrow ("Helm proposes"), the relation, and a footer with Accept and Reject. While the Studio is read-only the footer says "Continue to accept or reject." instead of the buttons. A proposed node, if one ever carries an answer, takes the same shape on the node itself.

**Cost he took:** a proposal whose edge is panned off screen is invisible until you pan to it, and nothing else points you there. No list, count or finder compensates.

**Where it landed:** note g0zl, on `studios/proposal-on-the-board`, #1660.
