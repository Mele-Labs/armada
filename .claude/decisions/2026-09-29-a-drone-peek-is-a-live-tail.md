# What does a peek into a Drone show?

**Decided 2026-09-29.**

The task panel held an empty frame for a peek into its Drone, waiting on the Drones work. Once that landed, the owner: *"We should honestly turn this card into something that can be used in other places. A little peek into the drone and what its doing and a messsage box to send a message to the drone."*

**Chosen:** a reusable Drone card. Its head names the Drone, its state and how long it ran, and opens the full Drone in the Drones destination. Under that sits a live tail: the last few turns of its transcript, the same rows the Drones sheet draws, following as it writes. The message box goes at its foot.

**Cost he took:** it is taller, about 200px, and pushes the task's other fields down.

**Where it landed:** `plan/task-dock`.
