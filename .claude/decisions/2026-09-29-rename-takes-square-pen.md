# The heading's Rename wants an edit icon, and every edit glyph is banned. Which one?

**Decided 2026-09-29.**

On an open Studio, the owner left a note on the heading's `Rename`: *"Turn this
into an edit icon button instead of a word button, so it would just be the edit
icon, and when you hover over it, a tool tip would show that says rename."* His
own registry from the day before stood in the way. `pencil` was reserved to the
sketch pad's pen, *"never edit, never rename"*, and the write-up row turned
down `square-pen` as a pen that collides with `pencil`. The canvas rail draws
that pencil on the same screen as the heading.

Offered `pencil` with its reservation lifted, `square-pen` minted for rename, or
a non-pen glyph such as `text-cursor-input`:

**Chosen:** `square-pen`, minted for rename and nothing else. `pencil` stays the
sketch pad's pen, and its reservation stays.

**Cost he took:** a pen sits near the rail's pencil, and at 16px the two read as
cousins. It lifts the ban his 28 Sep registry put on `square-pen`, for rename
only.

**Where it landed:** annotation `jyee`; `[icons.square-pen]` in
`packages/icons/icons.toml`; `StudioName`'s heading, on `studios/rename-icon`
(#1658).
