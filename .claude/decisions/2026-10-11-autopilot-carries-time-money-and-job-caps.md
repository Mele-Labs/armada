# What caps Autopilot as a whole?

**Decided 2026-10-11.**

Every Job already stops at its own dollar and turn ceiling (#51), so no single
Job can run away. Nothing adds them up, though: fourteen Jobs at $4 is $56 he
first sees in the morning, and #51's own finding is that a runaway is a
sequence of Jobs, not one Drone.

**Chosen:** all three caps, each optional, set when Autopilot starts: a time
window (*until 7am*), a dollar total, and a number of Jobs. Whichever is
reached first stops new picks.

**Cost he took:** three settings on Autopilot rather than one. Like #51's cap,
each stops what would start next and not what is already running, so
Autopilot can overshoot by as many Jobs as it runs at once.

**Where it landed:** not built.
