# Does a Job's thread show its reasoning?

**Decided 2026-11-10.** Yes. Each turn's reasoning is a folded row, and the one before a question asked with nothing
written to him since his message is drawn open above it.

Job 20b4e00a, asked to explain a question, worked the explanation out in its reasoning and asked again. He saw the
question twice and no explanation. Fleet had dropped every Job's reasoning text since 28 Aug 2026, when an agent fixing
#159 made the transcript decoder carry only that a turn reasoned. It filed whether a viewer should ever read the text as
an open question, `[observe-reasoning-content]`, and that question left `docs/OPEN.md` on 17 Oct 2026 without being put
to him. His words on finding it: *"I never agreed to that intentionally"*.

| Asked | He chose |
|---|---|
| How to fix a question asked with nothing written before it | Show the reasoning, over bringing back the question check he deleted on 6 Nov or a line in what every Job is told |
| Which reasoning to show | Every turn, folded, over only the reasoning before a question |

**What crosses is the CLI's own summary**, never the working, which the CLI keeps encrypted. Over all of Job 20b4e00a
that was 49 summaries and 11.7 KB beside a 1.26 MB thread, so the size that made the August agent hold it back does not
apply to it. `docs/concepts/session/a-terminal-jobs-thread.md` says how it is drawn.
