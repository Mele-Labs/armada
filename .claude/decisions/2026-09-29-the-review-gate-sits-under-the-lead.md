# Where does a person approve a Job, now that Overview leads with one thing?

**Decided 2026-09-29.** The other half of
`2026-09-29-overview-leads-with-what-releases-the-most.md`.

The reframe deleted the arrangement that held the review gate. The lead read
*Land is waiting on you to approve it. All 3 Checks passed and the Judge met
both criteria.* over a button called **Review it** that reached nothing — and
nothing anywhere else in Bridge could approve a Job. Record reads the ledger,
Workflow draws the run, and neither holds a verdict.

Offered four places: under the lead, on Record, in Workflow's step inspector,
or dropped as one of the things being replaced.

## Under the lead, where the sentence is

*"Under the lead, like the question."*

It is the same answer he gave an hour earlier for a Drone's question, and for
the same reason. An approval is rank 1 in the ranking above — **waiting on
you** — and rank 1 is answered where it is said, not one press away.

**Cost he took:** Overview grows a second heavy region on one status, and the
reframe's *one thing* is one thing plus its controls.

## The record folds under it

Not asked, decided in the build and recorded here. Unfolded, the verdict sheet
put *what you asked for*, *what the drone says it did*, the pull request, *what
proves it*, *what it left alone* and a figures row between the lead and the
buttons — the board's own cards were a screen and a half down on the one status
a person most needs to act on.

`VerdictSheet` already folds under Armada's own review, for the stated reason
that *the review already says what it found*. The lead says the same thing
first: its second clause is built from the same Checks and the same criteria.
So the record folds behind **The Job's record**, and the lead, the decision and
the board fit one screen.

**What this gives up:** the criteria are a sentence in the lead rather than a
row each, which is one press away from what the sketch drew.

**30 Sep 2026: the sentence above was not true when it was written.** The lead
read the waiting step alone, and a delivering step verifies nothing of its own —
so on `job/reviewAtDelivery`, the Job this fold most often sits under, the
second clause was empty while the folded record held three Checks and two
criteria. It is true now: the lead counts every step's evidence, which is
`the-lead-counts-the-whole-jobs-evidence`. The fold itself is unchanged.

**2 Oct 2026: the fold is gone, the owner's own reversal.** Shown three
arrangements of the record (#1680), he picked a card per section in the Settings
board's columns, and on the fold line itself wrote: *"I dont think we need this
to collapse anymore. We can probably just remove this panel."* So the record's
cards sit straight under the lead, open, at every gate, and there is no
**The Job's record** line to press. The cost named above is now paid: the board's
own cards sit below the record and the decision, not beside the lead.
