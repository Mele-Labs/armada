# How should a Job's asks reach him?

**Said 2026-11-19**, walking the native review sheet on Feature Catalog's items: "Its just a million different things and there is not one cohesive experience." He named the words he meets (Review Sheet, Drawing, Takes, Document, Sketches, Diagrams, Reading, Informs) and said he never knows where a press lands: the review sheet, the thread, a full-window viewer, or one item that is a drawing and takes at once. Pointing at a part's `DRAWING` caption: "This title is useless to me."

## What he chose

| Asked | He chose | The cost he took |
|---|---|---|
| The rule to rebuild around | One ask, one item, one place | Fleet and Bridge both change |
| Where a press on an asking card lands | The review sheet, on that item | The full-window viewer goes for asks |
| A drawing that explains takes | No explaining drawings: takes only, background as a line of text | Some context is lost |
| Something that only informs | Thread and Artifacts, never the sheet | Found by scrolling or in Artifacts |
| An answered item | Leaves the sheet at once; the answer stays on its thread card | No glance back in the sheet |
| A multiple-choice question | A sheet item like any other | The pop-up in the message box goes |
| Whose asks a sheet opened from a Job lists | Only that Job's; from the Jobs list, everyone's | Two scopes of one sheet |
| An item's title | The question in plain words; Fleet refuses an ask that is not one | Jobs must word a real question |
| Approve all | Removed | Each item is answered on its own |
