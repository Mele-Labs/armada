# What does Progress read as, and what does Not yet do?

**Decided 2026-10-22.** His words, on Progress in an open Job: *"It feels like
the tickets + PRs and then the group / tasks all flow into each other. Its just
a big list and its not immediately clear what am I looking at. The current group
gets the same treatment as unstarted tasks besides a little pulsing icon."*
Issues drew as cards and groups as equal cards, so the group being worked looked
like every group to come.

**Chosen: a running account written by a small model, Progress in three named
parts, and a Not yet on the plan.** On the first walk he said
*"No this walk completely missed the point."* What he wanted was an account of
his last message, and on where it goes he said
*"I thought you meant a panel over here in progress"*, so it is the top of Progress and not a panel of its own.

| Asked | He chose | The cost he took |
|---|---|---|
| Who writes the account | *"A small model Fleet runs"*, on `models.account`, haiku by default, rewritten at most every five seconds and kept until his next message | A model call every few seconds while a Session works, and wording a model can get wrong. A line that names a file, a path, a code identifier or a tool is dropped, so an answer can come back with nothing to show |
| Where it sits | The top of Progress, with the live view under it: the newest page the Session showed or served, as a scaled picture that a press opens | A picture is taken in a window never shown, so it lags the page by a few seconds |
| How Progress is laid out | **Zones**, of three takes: Spine and Folded were the others. Now is the account and the live view; Plan is a step list, a finished group one muted line, the group being worked open and raised with its tasks, a group to come one muted line; Issues is compact rows of issues and their pull requests. The workflow name sits in the panel's band beside the step rail | A group's title and subagent type are text until a pencil opens them |
| What the plan card reads first | A plain summary, one line a group of what it gives him, the groups under Details | The subagent types and paths are one press away |
| What Not yet does | Sets the plan aside, clears it from Needs you, and tells the agent *"Keep talking, no plan until I ask"* | The Session has no plan to approve until he asks for one |
| Presses | Pause, Resume, Auto land and Approve change the screen at the press and revert with Fleet's words on a refusal | For a moment the screen can say something Fleet has not agreed to |

**Rejected.** Spine and Folded, the other two Progress takes, and the first
walk.

**What it keeps.** The account is the one thing on a Session's activity that no
tool call detects, and nothing else in Progress is read from what an agent said:
the plan's states are still Fleet's observation. The account names the approved
plan group it belongs to and never a proposed one. `docs/concepts/session/working-on.md`
holds the rules of the account, `docs/concepts/session/in-bridge.md` what Bridge
draws.

**Revised 2026-10-22, later.** His words on the band: *"The header of the progress
bar is pretty jammed up with items."* Asked where the workflow picker and the step
rail should go, he chose a row under the band: the band keeps the label, Sketch,
Ask for an update and the approved mark, and the picker and the steps stand in one
quiet row at the top of the body, drawn only where a workflow exists. This replaces
"the workflow name sits in the panel's band beside the step rail" above.
