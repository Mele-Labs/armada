# Who hears a Judge's refusal when the panel agrees? (#206)

**Decided 2026-10-11.**

At 2am the Judge refuses a fix: *the test still sleeps 50ms, so it is less
flaky, not fixed.* Every refusal comes to him today, so a standing order's Job
stalls until morning. Asked whether that is acceptable for a standing order,
he chose to build #206 first, and then settled its three open questions.

**Chosen:**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What makes a refusal come to him anyway | The Judges themselves judging the work risky. A refusal that only says the work needs redoing goes back to the Drone |
| How many times back before it comes to him | After two retries |
| Is three calls to one model a second opinion | No. A panel's members can be different models |

**Cost he took:** risk is a Judge's call and not a list of paths, so it is only
as good as the Judges' sense of it. Up to two more Drone runs are spent on work
that may never pass. A panel member has its own model setting to build.

**Where it landed:** #206, which a standing order now waits on.
