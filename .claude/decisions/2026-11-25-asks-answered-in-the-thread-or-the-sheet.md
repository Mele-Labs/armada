# Where does he answer a Job's ask?

**Said 2026-11-25**, on the native Bridge after the sheet-only rule of `2026-11-19-one-ask-one-item-one-place.md` landed (#4030): "When a job has a question for me, I now need to click this bar, it opens then I need to click the question, then the panel opens and I can finally answer the question. I am feeling really disconnected." Asked which asks should open ready to answer in the thread: "having the quick cards in the thread was really nice. It was just the experience of getting to a consistent review sheet before that I really didn't like." The sheet "is nicer when I want to get focused and open everything in the review sheet. Especially multiple items for review."

## What he chose

| Asked | He chose | The cost he took |
|---|---|---|
| Where an ask is answered | On its card in the thread, or in the review sheet; either answers it | Two places draw one ask's answer |
| Which asks get a quick card in the thread | Every kind: a question, a drawing, a draft, a plan and a vote | The thread is longer while asks wait |
| A press on an ask in the Needs you bar | Goes to its card in the thread | The sheet is one more press away |
| How he reaches the sheet | Review all on the Needs you bar opens it on every open ask | None named |

It replaces the rows of the 19 Nov record on where a press lands and where an ask is answered. The rest of that record stands: one ask is one item titled with its question, an answered item leaves the list at once, explaining drawings never stand beside takes, and there is no Approve all.
