# How does he see how far a long Session has got?

**Decided 2026-10-14.** His words, from the Session that scoped it: *"We did a
lot of back and forth at the beginning around designing the new app. Now you
and subagents are off building it. I have no idea the progress you are
making."* He wanted a drone constructing something on the battlefield, and
asked whether that would cost too many tokens.

**Chosen: a ring under the drone.** Approved on the walk
`?walk=battlefieldProgress&build=ring&road=outer`, branch
`proto/battlefield-progress`, over a tower and a launch gantry.

| Asked | He chose | The cost he took |
|---|---|---|
| What "Review" is | Neither: what happens between built and merged varies by repository, so only Scope, Plan and Implement are fixed | No fixed six-phase line for a Session |
| How the road to main reads | Stops light as they happen, and the ones not reached are drawn faint from what that repository's recent Sessions did | A faint stop is a forecast |
| What grows the ring | A tick checked by edits: ticked with edits seen is solid, ticked with none is hollow, edits with no tick raise scaffold | Two kinds of piece to read |
| How stalled looks | Both: the amber ring at ten quiet minutes, and the unfinished part dimming the longer it sits | Two signals for one fact |
| What a landed structure does | Clears on land; what landed is in the Activity feed | Nothing on the field says what landed today |
| Where the road goes on the ring | A thinner ring of arcs outside the task ring, closed by the landing | |
| The mini view | An open Session swaps the Bays for its ring drawn tall, Incoming calls stay; a Job draws its ring in its own map beside the step that works the plan, and gets no band | Bays are out of sight while a Session is open |

**His idea on cost, kept.** The agent states its plan with the tools it already
calls (its task list, plan mode), the mod hears those calls, and Fleet tracks
the work against them from what it observes. Bridge draws the ring from Fleet's
records. No new agent tool, no prompt text, no model tokens. This keeps
`dont-trust-agents`: the plan only enriches, observed edits decide.

**What it keeps.** A Job's plan and tasks are unchanged; they are what its ring
is made of, and Fleet already ticks them on a step working a Drone per task.

**Routed elsewhere.** A terminal Session's permission prompt raises no call,
since the mod does not hear it. He sent that to the Cockpit Updates Session,
which owns Incoming calls.

**Asked to approve the scope, he pressed Just get it done**, which reached the
Session as *"Decide this yourself. Take the reasonable path and keep moving."*
He meant: *"just land the work, dont worry about formalities."* So it is built
and landed, not filed, in three stages: Fleet and the mod learn the plan and
the road (no wait), then Bridge draws the ring on the flight deck once #2301 is
in `main`. The button's line was reworded to say so in #2325.

## 15 Oct 2026: no list, no segments; the road in any phase; ask for an update

His words on the first live Session: *"This session ring is only showing half
filled and there is no outer ring."* Its record had no task list and the road's
stops in the scope phase.

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|

| With no task list the ring is one unsegmented band: a faint outline while scoping or planning, an even scaffold texture once edits are seen, solid when landed. The texture turns only while the drone is writing | A partial fill implies a fraction nobody measured |
