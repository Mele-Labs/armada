# How does Plan's lead take less of the screen?

**Decided 2026-09-29.**

*What this Job is held to* sat at the top of Plan at full height, and on a failed group the owner scrolled past it every time: *"This occupies so much vertical space of this tab."* On 28 Sep he had put it first, as the reason the plan exists.

**Chosen:** it stays first, collapsed to one line — the heading and the first criterion cut to fit — and a press opens the rest. It remembers which way it was left.

**Cost he took:** the criteria after the first are hidden until pressed.

**Where it landed:** `plan/task-dock`.
