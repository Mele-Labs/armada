How a Job lands

A Job is one piece of work an agent does in its own copy of the repository. This page follows one from your request to a merge on main: which part does what, what you are asked and when, and what checks the work before it lands. The diagrams are clickable.

Armada in eight words
Job
One piece of work, with its own thread, plan and slot.
Drone
The coding agent working a Job.
Slot
A worktree leased to one Job.
Gate
A fact that ends a stage, such as your Approve.
Walk
A screen change you click through before it lands.
Sketch
A drawing a Job shows you.
Document
A page a Job writes for you to read.
Check
A named test or lint that Fleet runs.
What you do
  • Say what you want in a Job's thread.
  • Approve the plan, and walk any screen it changes.
  • Answer questions as they come up in Needs you.
  • Nothing else. Armada merges once CI is green.

4 · What holds the work back

Rules

Each one stops a Job at a point, until something is true.

You

Plan first

Nothing is built until you approve the plan.

You

Walk what you see

A screen change waits until you walk it and approve it.

Store

Own copy

Each Job works in its own slot, so two agents never touch one tree.

Daemon

Checks before a pull request

Fleet refuses to open one until the format and type checks pass.

Outside

Green CI to merge

The merge queue lands it only on a green run.

Agent

Never idle

A Job either works, waits on you by name, or says it is done.
